Paired Conversion Changes

Why these inputs changed

You selected Binary and Paired. This means we are measuring the same users before and after a change.

In this situation, comparing overall conversion rates is not statistically valid. Instead, the analysis focuses only on users whose conversion status changed.

This method is called McNemar’s test. It is the standard and statistically correct approach for paired conversion data.

Users whose conversion status did not change do not need to be entered.
