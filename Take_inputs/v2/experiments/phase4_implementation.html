Implementation guidance & industry best practices

Show

What must NOT change

  • Sample size and traffic allocation
  • Primary metric definition
  • Experiment duration
  • Control vs test assignment logic
Changing any of the above invalidates statistical inference.

Randomization, stratification & contamination control

  • Assignment must be deterministic and stable per unit
  • Stratify only on pre-exposure variables (e.g. platform, region)
  • Never stratify on outcomes or post-exposure behavior
  • Avoid shared caches, state, or side effects across variants
No amount of analysis can recover validity once randomization or isolation is violated.

Common implementation mistakes

  • Early stopping based on partial results
  • Metric leakage across variants
  • Unequal exposure or delayed rollout
  • Changing logging or instrumentation mid-experiment

Engineering checklist

  • Verify exposure counts and balance
  • Ensure events fire before any metric evaluation
  • Confirm control metrics behave as expected
  • Monitor for unexpected control drift

Product checklist

  • Launch only when traffic is stable
  • Avoid peeking before planned completion
  • Stop only after planned sample or duration
